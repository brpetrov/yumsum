YumSum gives general estimates, not medical advice. Calorie targets come from standard formulas and the calories and protein shown for meals are estimates produced by an AI model from what you type, say or photograph. They can be wrong, sometimes by a lot.

Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet, and don't use the app if you are under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding, have or have had an eating disorder, or have a medical condition affected by diet, unless a professional has told you it's appropriate. The app's minimum calorie limits are safety floors, not recommendations. Stop and seek advice if you feel unwell.
