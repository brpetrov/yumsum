Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

LeanStreak is a calorie and weight tracker made by Boyan Petrov, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions and requests: broovenn@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and name. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see them. If you sign in with Google, we receive the email address and name of your Google account instead.
  • Profile: age, gender, height, current and target weight, activity level, training frequency and goal pace. These are used to calculate your calorie target.
  • What you log: meals (names, calories, protein and the time you logged them) and weigh-ins.
  • AI usage counts: how many AI estimates you have used each day, to apply the daily limit.
  • Technical data: Firebase records basic technical information such as IP address and device type for security and to operate the service.
  • Genuine-app checks: to stop others abusing the AI feature, every AI request carries a token from Google's App Check, which confirms it comes from a real copy of the app. On Android this uses Google Play Integrity; on the web it uses reCAPTCHA Enterprise, which looks at device and browser signals and may set cookies. Google runs these checks and tells us only whether the request is genuine. The Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply to them.
  • Packaged food lookups: when you point the camera at a packet's barcode, the phone reads the barcode itself and only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a non-profit open food database, to fetch the product's name and nutrition figures. When you search for a packaged food, the words you searched for are sent the same way. Nothing about you goes with either.
  • Products you contribute: if a barcode isn't on Open Food Facts and you choose to contribute it, the two photos you take of the packet (its front and its nutrition table) and the product details you check are sent to Open Food Facts and published there for anyone to use: the photos under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike licence, the details under the Open Database License. Alongside them go a random ID for this installation of the app, so Open Food Facts' volunteers can manage contributions, and, if the country in your profile is the UK or Bulgaria, that country as where the product is sold. Your name, email and account are never sent. Please keep people and personal details out of these photos. The same photos are also read by Google's Gemini API to fill in the details for you, as described below.
  • Crash reports: if the app on your phone crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a report with the device model, operating system version, app version and the technical trace of the crash, filed under a random installation ID. It contains nothing you logged.

Reminders are scheduled by the app on your device, and reminder settings, theme and other preferences stay there; none of this is sent to us. The app contains no advertising and no advertising or analytics trackers.

How your meal descriptions, photos and voice notes are processed

When you ask for an estimate, the text you typed, the photo you took or the voice note you recorded is sent to Google's Gemini API, which returns the calories, protein and a breakdown. When you contribute a product, its two photos are sent the same way so the label can be read for you. We keep only the resulting meal entry; we do not store your photos or recordings.

Google processes this content under its own terms. On the Gemini API tier LeanStreak uses, Google may use the content you send to improve its products. Please don't include personal details in meal descriptions, photos or recordings that you wouldn't want processed in that way.

Why we process your data

Only to run the app for you: to calculate and update your plan, show your history, send the reminders you turn on, and estimate meals you ask about. The legal basis is the contract between you and us (providing the app) and, for health-related details such as weight and diet, your consent, which you give by entering them and can withdraw by deleting them or your account.

Who else sees it

We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising. It is processed by these providers on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Hosting): stores your account and logs on Google Cloud servers, which may be outside the UK. Google provides the standard contractual safeguards for such transfers.
  • Google Gemini API: estimates meals, as described above.
  • Google App Check (Play Integrity on Android, reCAPTCHA Enterprise on the web): confirms requests come from the genuine app.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: receives crash reports from the phone apps.
  • Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org): answers barcode lookups and food searches, and publishes the products you choose to contribute. Its data is open under the Open Database License.

How long we keep it

Until you remove it. In Account Settings you can Reset all data (deletes every meal, summary, weigh-in and usage record but keeps your account) or Delete account (deletes everything, including your login). Deletion takes effect immediately in our database.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, or object to how it's processed. Most of this you can do yourself in the app; for anything else, email broovenn@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

LeanStreak is a dieting tool and is not intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app.
