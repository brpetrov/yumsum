Delete your YumSum account

YumSum, by Boyan Petrov · updated 9 November 2026

In the app

  1. Open the menu (top left) and choose Settings.
  2. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or with Google if you sign in with Google.

Your account and data are deleted straight away, and you're signed out.

Without the app

Email help.yumsum@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my YumSum account". We delete the account and its data within a month and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile: name, email, age, gender, height, weights and plan.
  • Everything you logged: meals, daily summaries, weigh-ins and days off.
  • AI Coach notes, your daily AI and past-day-change counts, and your YumSum Star record with its links to Google Play purchases.

What isn't

  • A YumSum Star subscription: deleting your account doesn't cancel it. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Feedback you sent: it stays as an email until we no longer need it. Ask us at the address above to delete it.
  • Products you contributed to Open Food Facts: their photos and details are published there under open licences and carry nothing about you.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days, then deleted.

Only your data, not the account

Settings → Reset all data deletes every meal, summary, weigh-in, day off and AI Coach note, and keeps your account and plan.
