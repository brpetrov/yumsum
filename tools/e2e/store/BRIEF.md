# Play Store screenshots: the brief

*For the designer or agent making YumSum's Google Play screenshots. Written 10 November 2026. Everything you need is in this folder: the raw screens (`en-*.png`, 1080 × 1920, taken from the app), the competitor references (`reference/`), the app icon (`../../../assets/android/play_store_512.png`) and the feature graphic (`feature-graphic.png`).*

## The job

Make 6 to 8 phone screenshots, 1080 × 1920 PNG, each under 8 MB, saved here as `play-1.png` … `play-8.png` in the order below. They must **sell**: a Play visitor decides in about three seconds, from the icon, the name and the first two screenshots. Every screenshot is a poster, not a screen grab: a big headline, one short line under it, and the real app screen in a phone frame. Look at `reference/` to see what works in this category; make ours cleaner and warmer, in the app's own look.

## Who we're selling to, and what makes them tap

1. **People who gave up on calorie counting because logging was a chore**: searching a database, weighing everything. Trigger: *seconds, not minutes*. Show the quick-log bar, the mic, the sparkle.
2. **People who just want the number**: "tell me the calories". Trigger: *AI does the counting*, and it's **free: 10 estimates a day, no ads**. Most apps lock AI behind a subscription; we don't. Say it as our own fact, never as a comparison with named apps.
3. **Gym-goers and protein counters**. Trigger: *protein first*, the rings, "140 g" in gold.
4. **People burnt by paywalls and ads**. Trigger: *free to use, no ads*, and the YumSum Star extras named honestly.
5. **Bulgarians**: the app is in Bulgarian too (one slide at the end).

## The look

- **Background**: the app's cream, `#FAF2E7`, flat or with a very soft warm gradient. A faded food photo behind the phone is fine on one or two slides (as in the references), but never behind the headline.
- **Headline**: Fredoka, bold, dark brown `#2B1F18`, two lines at most, at least 96 px tall per line at 1080 wide, so it reads in the thumbnail strip. **Line under it**: Nunito, `#76665A`, about 48 px. Both fonts are on Google Fonts; the app uses them.
- **Accent**: orange `#C94D1A` for one word or a pill badge per slide (for example a pill reading "10 a day, free"); green `#1E9E6A` only for "on track" things. Don't introduce other colours.
- **Phone frame**: a plain dark rounded frame, straight or tilted no more than 6°, taking 60 to 70% of the height, screen cropped from the raw PNG at full resolution (never upscaled). The raw screens have no status bar; leave it off.
- **Consistency**: same background, headline position and frame size on every slide. The set should read as one strip.
- A small YumSum icon in a corner of slide 1 only.

## Rules (Play policy and the app's own voice)

- No ratings, awards, "#1", "best", "most accurate", star badges or review quotes. Nothing like the "4.6 Play Store" badge in `reference/ref-32.png`.
- No competitor names, logos or "unlike other apps".
- Every claim is true of the app today: 10 AI estimates a day free (typed or spoken), 30 with YumSum Star; plate photos, Adaptive formula, AI Coach and detailed macros are YumSum Star; recipes, barcode scanning, the mic and typed logging are free. Label a YumSum Star feature as such on its slide.
- Copy in British English, sentence case, no exclamation marks, "you". Short: a headline is five words or fewer.
- Only real screens of the app. No invented UI, no mock data drawn by hand, no stock food in the phone frame.
- Don't show the name "Tester" or the test account's details.

## Deliver

`play-1.png` … `play-N.png` in this folder, plus a `play-contact.png` strip (all slides side by side, any size) for a quick look. Say which slides were skipped and why.
